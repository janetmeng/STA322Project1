# STA 322 Project 1: Characteristics of Trinity College Faculty

**Janet Meng and Amy Duan**   
**STA 322: Study Design, Duke University**     
**Fall 2026**

We designed and ran a stratified random sample with proportional allocation of 100 faculty in Duke's Trinity College of Arts & Sciences to estimate the fraction who are women, the fraction with a grant ongoing in 2026, the average years since the Ph.D., and whether women and men differ in the fraction who are full professors.

## Where to find things

- **Report:** `report.qmd` (render with `quarto render report.qmd`)
- **Code:** `analysis.Rmd` and the appendix of `report.qmd`
- **Data:** `faculty_counts.csv` (sampling frame), `sample_ids.csv` (drawn sample), `data.csv` (collected data)
